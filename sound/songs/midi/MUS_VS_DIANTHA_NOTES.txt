This original provisional theme uses a B minor center, 176 BPM, 4/4,
a 4-bar intro and a 48-bar looping form (MIDI markers [ and ]).
10 tracks (including tempo): square-wave lead, glockenspiel,
Steinway piano, strings, synth bass, pizzicato, flute/choir, harp
and percussion. It uses existing samples.
